Our Story

Passion behind every petal

Charlotte Celebrations was born from a deep love of bringing people together. Founded with the belief that every celebration — no matter the size — deserves to be beautiful, meaningful, and stress-free.

Our team of passionate stylists, florists, and culinary artists work together seamlessly to deliver cohesive, spectacular events that reflect you.

  • Personalised consultations from first contact to final clean-up
  • Locally sourced florals and seasonal, fresh ingredients
  • Trusted vendor network for lighting, photography, and entertainment
  • Flexible packages for every budget and vision
  • Based in Johannesburg, serving all of Gauteng and beyond
